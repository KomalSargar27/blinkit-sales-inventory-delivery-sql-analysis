# 🛒 Blinkit Sales, Inventory & Delivery Performance Analysis using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit sales, inventory, delivery performance, pricing, discounts, profitability, and customer ratings using SQL.

The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Calculate total revenue and estimated profit.
- Analyze inventory levels and identify products below their reorder level.
- Identify high-demand products with low stock.
- Evaluate delivery performance across different cities.
- Analyze the impact of discounts on product sales.
- Explore customer ratings across different product categories.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Exploration & Cleaning

- Total number of records
- Total number of unique products
- Product categories
- Missing value checks

### 2. Sales Analysis

- Total quantity sold
- Total sales revenue
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Category-wise revenue analysis
- Category-wise quantity sold

### 3. Profit Analysis

- Estimated total profit
- Category-wise profit analysis
- Top 10 most profitable products

### 4. Inventory Analysis

- Products below reorder level
- High-demand products with low stock
- Inventory risk categorization

### 5. Delivery Analysis

- On-time vs delayed deliveries
- Cities with the highest delayed deliveries
- Average delivery time by city

### 6. Discount & Pricing Analysis

- Average discount by category
- Top 10 most discounted products
- Analysis of discounts and sales quantity

### 7. Customer Rating Analysis

- Average rating by category
- Highly rated products with low sales
